## CAMPAIGN A: "The Hidden Problem" (Awareness — Facebook/Nextdoor/Instagram)

### Prompt 1 — Crawlspace Discovery (Hero Image)
> Create a realistic image of a dark, damp residential crawlspace viewed from a basement access hatch. The homeowner is a man in his 40s looking down into the crawlspace with a worried expression, holding a flashlight that illuminates standing water and visible mold on wooden joists. The crawlspace has exposed dirt floor, old fiberglass insulation hanging down, and moisture beading on pipes. The lighting is dramatic — warm light from the house above contrasts with the cold dampness below. The feeling is 'I had no idea this was under my house.' Professional quality, photorealistic.

### Prompt 2 — Foundation Crack (Outdoor Discovery)
> Create a realistic image of a well-maintained Virginia Beach suburban home exterior, focusing on the foundation area at ground level. There is a visible stair-step crack in the brick facade. A homeowner's hand is pointing at the crack with concern. The lawn is green and manicured, and a white work van is parked in the driveway. The background shows a typical Hampton Roads neighborhood with mature trees. The feeling is a homeowner noticing something wrong with their home. Natural daylight, photorealistic.

### Prompt 3 — Musty Smell / Air Quality
> Create a realistic image of a mother and young child sitting in a living room. The mother has a concerned expression, holding a tissue. The room looks clean and well-decorated, but the air feels heavy. In the corner of the room, a floor vent is visible — hinting that the air quality issue is coming from below. The feeling is 'why is my family always sick?' The crawlspace below is implied but not shown. Soft natural lighting, warm but slightly moody atmosphere, photorealistic.

## CAMPAIGN C: "Before & After" (Proof — Facebook/Instagram/Google Display)

### Prompt 6 — Crawlspace BEFORE (The Problem)
> Create a realistic image of a badly neglected residential crawlspace. Key elements: exposed dirt floor with puddles of standing water, black mold growing on wooden floor joists above, old fiberglass insulation falling down and touching the wet ground, visible pest droppings and insect activity, rusty pipes with moisture condensation dripping. A measuring tape or moisture meter is visible showing a high reading. The lighting is dim with a bright camera flash illuminating the damage. Gritty, shocking, eye-opening. Photorealistic.

### Prompt 7 — Crawlspace AFTER (The Solution)
> Create a realistic image of a clean, professionally encapsulated residential crawlspace. Key elements: bright clean white vapor barrier covering the entire floor with professionally taped seams, walls sealed with rigid foam insulation, a high-quality dehumidifier installed, a clean French drain system with a sump pump in the corner, bright LED lighting illuminating the entire space, a wireless humidity sensor on the wall showing 45% humidity. The feeling is clean, dry, professional, safe. Bright, well-lit, photorealistic.

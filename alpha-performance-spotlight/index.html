Meet Alpha Performance, Virginia Beach's Athletic Training Home

When Robert Melendez talks about training, he starts with the feet. Not in a casual way, but because he genuinely believes that everything you build in the gym has to start from the ground up. That philosophy runs through every session at Alpha Performance Fitness and Athletics, a private training facility tucked into the Lynnhaven area of Virginia Beach where the focus is less on machines and more on movement.

Robert has been training people in the Hampton Roads area for nearly a decade now. He did not stumble into this. He knew early on that fitness was what he was meant to do, and after years of working for someone else, he took the leap and built something of his own. The result is a 3,700 square foot facility with an open warehouse layout, twenty yards of turf, squat racks, dumbbells, barbells, and exactly zero machines. The space itself tells you what kind of gym this is before you ever touch a weight.

What to Expect When You Walk Into Alpha Performance

You are not walking into a big box gym. There is no sea of cardio equipment, no rows of cable machines, and nobody hiding behind headphones pretending the rest of the room does not exist. Alpha Performance feels more like a team training facility than a commercial gym, because that is what it was built to be. The energy is focused but encouraging. One reviewer, a competitive bodybuilder, put it plainly: the workouts here challenge you every single time you walk through the door, and the atmosphere makes you want to keep coming back.

That atmosphere comes from the top down. Robert runs the floor himself alongside his wife Justyce and a small team of trainers. When clients describe the experience, the word that keeps surfacing is personal. Robert creates individualized plans. He adjusts on the fly. He pays attention to how you move before he adds weight to the bar. Another client shared that working with Robert and following a plan he built produced real, visible results, and that the good vibes inside the gym are as much a draw as the training itself.

Athletic Performance Training for Every Age and Level

Alpha Performance was built as an athletic gym first, and that identity shapes everything they offer. Youth athletes have two dedicated class tracks. The Young Bucks program serves kids ages eight through twelve, teaching foundational movement and athletic skills in a structured but energetic environment. For older athletes, the Alpha Athletics class provides more advanced training geared toward middle school, high school, and even collegiate competitors who want to improve speed, strength, and agility.

Adults are not left out of the equation. The fitness side of the gym runs HIIT style classes, small group and semi private training, and fully personalized one on one coaching. The structure is flexible by design. You can mix and match packages, choose between unlimited monthly memberships and drop in rates, or commit to a personal training pack that scales with your goals. Pricing for classes lands between roughly $99 and $200 per month, while personal training packs range from a single session to bulk packages that reward consistency.

A Gym That Gives Back to Virginia Beach

There is a second name attached to Alpha Performance that says a lot about the kind of business Robert and Justyce are building. The Alpha Movement is their community focused arm, and it has already produced some genuinely impressive results. Earlier this year, the gym hosted Paddles with Purpose, a charity pickleball tournament that brought together local businesses, sponsors like Club Pilates and Lineage, and a room full of people who wanted to move for a good cause. Proceeds went to Lynnhaven River Now.

They also ran a movement challenge with a simple premise: tag Alpha Performance in your workout on social media, and every post counted toward a collective goal. When the community hit 200 workouts, Robert and Justyce donated $600 to Virginia Beach public schools. That kind of initiative does not come from a marketing playbook. It comes from genuinely wanting to be part of the city they serve.

Why Word of Mouth Still Builds the Best Gyms

Robert will tell you that his best source of new clients is the oldest one in the book. People tell their friends. One client shared that they never would have been motivated to attend boot camp sessions if a friend had not recommended Alpha Performance. That referral brought them in, the experience kept them coming back, and now their son trains there privately too, with results that have made a noticeable difference in his athletic performance.

Robert and Justyce stay active on Instagram and Facebook, and they run selective ads when the timing is right, but the engine that drives Alpha Performance is the quality of what happens inside those walls. When a bodybuilder, a busy parent, and a high school athlete all walk away saying the same thing about a gym, you know the standard is consistent.

Built by Someone Who Still Shows Up

Robert did not build Alpha Performance from a distance. He still trains clients, still runs the classes, and still handles the bulk of the behind the scenes work. His reason for owning his own business is refreshingly direct: he loves what he does and he does not much care for working under someone else. Nearly ten years into his training career, he wakes up early, trains people, takes a break in the afternoon, comes back and trains more people in the evening, and would not trade it for anything.

That is the kind of owner you want behind a gym. Someone who is in it every day, who knows your name, who notices when your form improves and when you need a push. Someone who built the place because he could not imagine doing anything else.

Alpha Performance Fitness and Athletics is a private gym that earns its reputation one session at a time. Whether you are a young athlete building a foundation, an adult looking for a training home that actually challenges you, or a parent wanting a coach who will invest in your child's development, this is a place worth walking into. The first step, as Robert would tell you, starts with your feet.

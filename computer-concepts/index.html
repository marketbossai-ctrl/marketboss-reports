❌ What's Broken

❌ No H1 on homepage — Critical SEO error. Every page needs exactly one H1.

❌ Broken title tag — "Hampton Roads Authorized Apple & PC Computer Sales & RepairLocator" — "Locator" is appended incorrectly

❌ 0 alt text on images — 32 images on homepage, 21 on each location page — all have alt="". Zero accessibility, zero SEO value.

❌ No schema markup — No LocalBusiness, no Service, no FAQ schema on any page. Google can't understand your business from your own site.

❌ No individual service pages — One combined "In-Store Mac & PC Repair" page instead of dedicated pages for screen repair, data recovery, upgrades, etc.

❌ No Google Maps embed — Missing on location pages and contact areas

❌ No sitemap/robots.txt accessible — WAF blocking, but no evidence of proper sitemap submission

❌ No meta description on blog — Recent News page has no meta description

❌ Inconsistent GBP website URLs — Yorktown links to location page, Williamsburg links to root domain

Part 4: The 7 Objections

1. "I'll just wait and see if it gets worse"

Reality: It will get worse. Foundation issues don't self-correct.

"That's fair. The good news is we caught it early. Here's what will happen if you wait 6 months vs. fixing it now — and here's the cost difference."

2. "I've got three quotes and they're all different"

"I expect that. Let me show you exactly what each quote is proposing — the method, materials, warranty — so you can compare apples to apples."

3. "I can't afford that right now"

"I understand. That's exactly why we offer financing. Let me show you what the monthly payment looks like — it's probably less than your electric bill."

4. "I'll just DIY it"

"You definitely could patch the crack. Here's what the patch will do — and here's what it won't do. The crack is the symptom, not the problem. If you don't fix the cause..."

5. "My neighbor had his done for half that"

"Can I take a look at what was done so I can tell you exactly what the difference is? Did they get a warranty? Was it the same system?"

6. "Do I really need encapsulation?"

"Here's what happens without it: your wooden floor joists sit in 70%+ humidity 24/7. That moisture is costing you money in higher energy bills and breaking down your home's structure. Encapsulation pays for itself in about 18 months just in energy savings."

7. "Fix it before I list the house"

"I can do that. But here's what I'd recommend if you want the sale to go through without the buyer coming back for more. Let me show you both options."

How to Give Market Boss AI Access to Your Google Business Profile

This takes 2 minutes. Follow the steps below.

Step 1: Open Google Business Profile

Go to business.google.com and sign in with the Google account that manages your business listing.

Step 2: Select Your Business

If you have multiple profiles, click on the business you want to give us access to.

Step 3: Open Users & Permissions

  • Click "Users" or "People and access" in the left sidebar menu
  • You may need to click "More" or the three-dot menu (⋮) to find it

Step 4: Add a New User

  • Click the "Add" button (blue button, top right area)
  • In the popup, enter: jeff@vbspotlight.com
  • For permission level, select: "Manager" (NOT Owner)

Step 5: Send Invitation

  • Click "Invite" or "Send"
  • Google will send an email to jeff@vbspotlight.com
  • That's it! We'll accept the invitation within 24 hours.

⚠️ Important Notes

  • Do NOT give Owner access — Manager is sufficient and keeps you as the sole owner
  • If you use a GBP management tool (Yext, SOCi, etc.), we may need additional access there too
  • If you don't see the "Users" option, you may need to verify your business first

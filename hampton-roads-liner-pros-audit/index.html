What's Missing / Broken

  • ❌ META DESCRIPTIONS — No meta description on any page. Google has no control over what appears in the search snippet below the title.
  • ❌ SINGLE-PAGE SITE — Despite sitemap listing 6 URLs, they are Wix boilerplate (contact form, finance app, gallery stub). The site is effectively a single page with everything crammed onto it.
  • ❌ NO SERVICE PAGES — Zero dedicated pages for pool liner replacement, pool building, paver work, or concrete. Everything is on the home page in a few paragraphs.
  • ❌ NO LOCATION PAGES — Serves all of Hampton Roads but has no pages for Virginia Beach, Norfolk, Chesapeake, Portsmouth, or any individual city. A "pool liner replacement Norfolk VA" search will never find this site.
  • ❌ TWO H1 TAGS — The home page has two H1 elements ("Introducing Hampton Roads Liner Pros" and "Subscribe Form"). Every page needs exactly one H1.
  • ❌ NO BLOG / CONTENT — Zero blog posts, zero articles, zero content strategy. No way to rank for informational queries like "how much does a pool liner cost" or "when to replace your vinyl liner."
  • ❌ STALE CONTENT — Copyright still says 2018. The sitemap was last touched in March 2026, but there has been no meaningful content expansion.
  • ❌ NO REVIEW SCHEMA — LocalBusiness schema exists but has no aggregate rating. Customer reviews are not marked up for star ratings in search results.
  • ❌ MISSING REVIEWS ON SITE — No embedded Google reviews, no testimonial section beyond the schema placeholder. Social proof is invisible to Google.
  • ❌ NO HOURS IN SCHEMA — The LocalBusiness JSON-LD has no opening hours. This is a required field for Google to display hours in search results and Maps.
  • ❌ WEAK ALT TEXT — Images have alt attributes but the text is generic ("IMG_4729.jpeg", "Untitled"). No descriptive keywords. Missed opportunity for Google Image search traffic.
  • ❌ NO FAQ SCHEMA — No FAQ markup. Competitors with FAQ schema can capture featured snippets for questions like "how long does a vinyl pool liner last."
  • ❌ NO EMAIL CAPTURE — No newsletter, no lead magnet, no way to capture a visitor's email and follow up.

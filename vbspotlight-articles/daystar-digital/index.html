Daystar Digital: Building Modern Websites for Small Businesses

Wilson County Spotlight · Austin Hayes · October 2026

Daystar Digital is Austin Hayes — a software developer who got tired of watching small businesses pay thousands for simple websites. So he started building them himself, for a fraction of the cost, with no runaround.

How Daystar Digital Started

Austin kept watching friends and family get quoted thousands of dollars by agencies for something simple — a website to sell their crafts, collect emails, and look legitimate online. He knew he could build the same thing in a couple of hours. So he did.

"Wix and other DIY builders are fine, but most people don't have the time or patience to spend days fighting with a website when they could be running their business," Austin says. With years of experience as a software developer and a background in IT support, building and fixing things for people is just what he does.

How He Works

Austin runs Daystar Digital the way he would want to be treated: honestly, with no gimmicks and no unnecessary upsells. He is a Christian, and to him that means doing right by people even when it costs him a sale. If he can genuinely help a business and make a fair living doing it, that is a win.

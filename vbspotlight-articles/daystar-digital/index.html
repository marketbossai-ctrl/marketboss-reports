Why Daystar Digital Started

It started with family and friends. Austin watched people he cared about getting quoted thousands by agencies for something simple. A website to sell their crafts. A page to collect emails. Something to make them look legitimate online so customers would take them seriously.

The prices agencies were charging did not match what the work actually required. Austin had spent years working as a software developer, and before that in IT support. He knew he could build the same thing in a couple of hours. So he did.

But he also knew that most people do not want to spend days fighting with a Wix template or a WordPress builder. They want to run their business. They want a website that works, looks professional, and does not drain their time or their bank account.

That is the gap Daystar Digital fills. Austin builds fast, modern websites for small businesses who just want something that works, without the agency price tag or the runaround.

The 6-D Process

Austin follows a structured process called the 6-D Process. It starts with Discovery, where he learns about the business, its goals, and its audience to build a strong foundation. Then comes Define, where he creates a clear strategy tailored to that business's growth and long-term success.

The third step is Design. He crafts a clean, professional design that reflects the brand and converts visitors into customers. Then Develop, where he builds the site using modern, scalable technology that will hold up as the business grows.

Deploy comes next. He launches the project smoothly with everything optimized and ready to go. The final step is Deliver, where he provides ongoing support, updates, and improvements as the business continues to grow.

What makes this process different is that it does not end at launch. Most developers hand over the keys and walk away. Austin stays on for support, because he knows small business owners do not have time to figure out why their site broke or how to update a page.

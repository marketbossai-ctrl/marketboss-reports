Jim's Pressure Washing: One Navy Sailor's Leap Into Business Ownership

How a young service member traded his doubts for a pressure washer and built something of his own in Virginia Beach.

A Decision Made in an Afternoon

There comes a moment in every entrepreneur's story where thinking stops and action begins. For Jimuel Alagon, that moment arrived in front of a YouTube tutorial. He had been watching videos about starting a pressure washing business for days, maybe weeks. He had all the usual reasons not to start. He did not have enough money. He did not know if he would fail. He was not sure he was ready. Then one creator said something that cut through all of it: turn off the video, go buy a pressure washer, and start. So he did. That same day, Jim bought a machine and cleaned his own driveway. He found it so satisfying that he kept going until the sun went down.

That was just over a year ago. Today, Jim's Pressure Washing serves homeowners across Virginia Beach, Norfolk, Chesapeake, and Portsmouth with house washing, driveway cleaning, gutter cleaning, patio cleaning, and fence cleaning. Jim is still serving full time in the United States Navy, which means every job he takes happens after duty hours or on weekends. His customers consistently mention two things: he shows up exactly when he says he will, and he does not cut corners.

From the Philippines to Virginia Beach

Jim came to the United States five years ago. His wife, a nurse, was accepted to work in the US, and he came with her. He studied criminal justice in the Philippines and was preparing to apply for the police force when life took a different direction. The Navy became his new path, and Virginia Beach became home. His first duty station is still his current one, and he plans to stay here until he retires. The same discipline that serves him in the military carries over into every job he takes. He shows up 15 minutes early. He communicates clearly. He treats every customer's property like his own.

"I always want to do the right thing. If I have a job and I know it is going to take three hours to get it clean, I take my time. I make sure I do not damage any vegetation. I get it done the right way."

What Customers Experience

When Jim pulls up to a house, the first thing you notice is the care he takes in setting up. He does not rush. He walks the property first, checking for delicate plants, sensitive surfaces, and any areas that need special attention. His soft washing technique is gentle enough for the most vulnerable siding but strong enough to remove years of built up grime and green mold. Customers who have hired him for driveway cleaning often remark that the results look better than when the concrete was first poured. He does not just spray and leave. He works each section methodically until it meets his standard.

His approach to gutter cleaning is equally thorough. Instead of just clearing the visible debris, he makes sure the downspouts are flowing freely and that water has a clear path away from the foundation. For fence cleaning, he adjusts his pressure and chemical mix based on the material, knowing that what works on a vinyl fence could damage a wooden one. That attention to detail is what turns one time customers into regulars.

The Reviews That Tell the Story

With 16 five star reviews on his Google Business Profile, the pattern is consistent. Customers talk about his punctuality, his fair pricing, and the quality of his work. One reviewer noted that he showed up exactly when he said he would and left the driveway looking better than expected. Another pointed out how careful he is around plants and landscaping, a concern every homeowner shares when hiring a pressure washing company. A third appreciated that he took the time to explain what he was doing and why, instead of just going through the motions.

His Filipino customers in particular have become loyal repeat clients. There is a trust that forms when you know the person doing the work comes from a place of honesty and integrity. Jim does not upsell. He does not pressure. He gives a fair price, does the work right, and asks only one thing: if you are happy with the result, leave a Google review. That simple approach has built his business from zero to a steady stream of referrals in just over a year.

Building Something That Lasts

Jim is focused on the long game. He spends more of his resources on marketing than on equipment upgrades because he understands that no amount of gear matters if nobody knows you exist. He is learning sales, learning marketing, and refining his craft one house at a time. His goal is to scale this business into something that can support his family when he eventually leaves the military. He wants to be present for his future children in a way that deployments would not allow.

Five years from now, he sees a company that has grown through focus, quality, and steady progress. He does not worry about the competition. He does not get distracted by what other pressure washing companies are doing. His attention is on his own growth, his own customers, and his own standard of work. In a world where too many businesses promise more than they deliver, Jim's Pressure Washing is a reminder that showing up, doing the right thing, and caring about the result still matters.

Ready for a Cleaner Home?

If your driveway, house, or fence is overdue for a cleaning, reach out to Jim's Pressure Washing. Fair prices, honest work, and results that speak for themselves.

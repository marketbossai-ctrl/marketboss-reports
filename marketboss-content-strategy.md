## SECTION 3: THE NEIGHBORHOOD CONTENT PLAYBOOK

This is your biggest opportunity. Here's the exact format to replicate per neighborhood:

**Title Pattern:**
`"[Service] in [Neighborhood], [City] — Why Most [Service Companies] Miss This Local SEO Opportunity"`

**Content Structure:**
1. Hook: What makes this neighborhood unique
2. Data point: "Searching [service] near [neighborhood] returns X results on Google Maps"
3. The opportunity: "Most [service companies] in [city] claim to serve everywhere, but Google rewards specificity"
4. How MarketBoss AI helps: Specific, neighborhood-focused strategy
5. CTA: Schedule a consultation

**Example:**
> *"Plumber SEO in Ghent, Norfolk — Why Most Plumbing Companies Miss Norfolk's Most Profitable Neighborhood"*
>
> Ghent is one of Norfolk's densest neighborhoods — historic homes, high foot traffic, homeowners who pay for quality work. Yet most plumbers in Norfolk use 'serving Norfolk' as their entire local SEO strategy. Google rewards specificity. A landing page optimized for 'plumber Ghent Norfolk VA' with Ghent-specific content, local photos, and neighborhood citations will outrank a generic plumber website every time.

## SECTION 5: THE CORNERSTONE CONTENT STRATEGY

Instead of writing 50 titles randomly, I recommend a **3-pillar structure**:

### Pillar 1: The Neighborhood Domination Library
**Goal:** Own every meaningful neighborhood in Hampton Roads for every relevant service
**Format:** Repeating template per neighborhood
**How to produce:** Use the same structure, swap neighborhood + service. Can be partially automated.
**SEO benefit:** Low-competition keywords, high conversion intent

### Pillar 2: The Trust Content Funnel
**Goal:** Answer every question a skeptical business owner types into Google
**Format:** Deep, authoritative, non-salesy guides
**How to produce:** One per week, focused on overcoming specific objections
**SEO benefit:** High-intent keywords, positions you as the authority

### Pillar 3: The Case Study Library
**Goal:** Prove it works with real numbers
**Format:** Before/after, anonymized if needed
**How to produce:** Document every client win from month 1
**SEO benefit:** Converts readers into leads

What's Missing / Broken

  • ❌ TITLE TAGS — Every page is missing a <title> tag. This is the single most important on-page SEO element. Google has nothing to display in search results.
  • ❌ META DESCRIPTIONS — Every page is missing a meta description. No control over what Google shows in the snippet below the title.
  • ❌ H1 HEADINGS — No page has a single H1. H2s are also absent. Zero heading hierarchy means Google has no way to understand what each page is about.
  • ❌ STRUCTURED DATA — No JSON-LD, no LocalBusiness schema, no FAQ schema, no Product schema. Zero. Google cannot display rich results (star ratings, phone, hours, service area) in search.
  • ❌ TEXT CONTENT — The homepage has ~222 characters of visible text — essentially nothing. Googlebot can render JavaScript, but the content is so thin there's nothing to rank.
  • ❌ JS RENDERING — All content depends on Duda's JavaScript loader. While Google CAN render JS, it's slower, uses more crawl budget, and is fragile. Screaming Frog, Ahrefs, and other SEO tools see blank pages.
  • ❌ NO BLOG / CONTENT — Zero blog posts, zero articles, zero content strategy. The site is a static brochure with no way to attract organic traffic through informational queries.
  • ❌ NO SCHEMA MARKUP — No FAQ schema, no review schema, no service schema. Competitors with schema will outrank on every query.
  • ❌ REVIEWS NOT VISIBLE — The reviews page exists but reviews are JS-rendered. Google may not see them. Reviews are not schema-marked up so they can't show star ratings in search results.
  • ❌ STALE CONTENT — Contact page hasn't been touched since August 2022 (4 years). Homepage last updated March 2025 (5 months ago). Portfolio was updated June 2025.
  • ❌ ONLY 6 PAGES — Tiny site footprint. Competitors with 20+ pages of service content, location pages, and blog posts will dominate.
  • ❌ NO LOCATION PAGES — They serve the entire Hampton Roads area but have zero location-specific pages.
  • ❌ NO ALT TEXT — Images have no alt attributes. Massive missed opportunity for image search traffic.
  • ❌ NO SOCIAL PROOF — Google reviews not found in search results. No embedded Google review widget. No testimonial schema on the site.

The Fix List

  • ImmediateTitle Tags — Every page needs a unique, keyword-rich title tag (55-65 chars). Homepage example: "Shower Doors & Custom Glass Etching | Brilliant Glass | Virginia Beach, VA"
  • ImmediateMeta Descriptions — Compelling 150-160 char description with a call to action on every page. Example: "Virginia Beach's trusted glass contractor. Custom shower enclosures, office partitions, mirrors, and glass etching. Free quotes. Call (757) 271-3060."
  • ImmediateH1 + Heading Structure — Exactly one H1 per page, H2s for sub-sections. Homepage H1 example: "Custom Shower Doors & Glass Etching in Virginia Beach"
  • ImmediateLocalBusiness Schema — Add JSON-LD with business name, address, phone, hours, service area (Virginia Beach, Norfolk, Chesapeake), aggregate rating, and SameAs social links.
  • This WeekService Pages — Break "Services" into individual pages: /shower-enclosures-virginia-beach, /custom-glass-mirrors-virginia-beach, /glass-etching-virginia-beach, /office-glass-partitions-virginia-beach. Each with 300+ words, unique title, schema, and before/after photos.
  • This WeekLocation Pages — Create pages for each city served: /glass-contractor-virginia-beach-va, /shower-doors-norfolk-va, /glass-installation-chesapeake-va. Each with unique content, local schema, and embedded Google Map.
  • This WeekReview Strategy — Claim and optimize Google Business Profile; embed Google reviews; add Review schema for star ratings; generate 2-3 new reviews per month minimum.
  • OngoingContent Strategy — Blog posts: "How to choose the right shower door," "Glass etching vs. frosted film," "5 signs your shower door needs replacement," "Commercial glass partitions: privacy vs. open plan." 1-2 posts per month minimum.
  • OngoingImage SEO — Descriptive alt text on every portfolio image; compress images; use descriptive filenames (shower-door-frameless-virginia-beach.jpg).

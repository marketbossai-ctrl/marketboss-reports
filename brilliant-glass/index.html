  • ❌ H1 HEADINGS — No page has a single H1. Zero heading hierarchy.
  • ❌ STRUCTURED DATA — No JSON-LD, no schema of any kind. Zero.
  • ❌ CONTENT — Homepage has ~222 characters of visible text — essentially nothing. 6 total pages for a business serving all of Hampton Roads.
  • ❌ JS RENDERING — All content depends on Duda's JavaScript loader.
  • ❌ NO BLOG — Zero blog posts, zero articles, zero content strategy.

What's Missing / Broken

  • ❌ META DESCRIPTIONS — Every page is missing a meta description.
  • ❌ H1 HEADINGS — No page has a single H1. Zero heading hierarchy.
  • ❌ STRUCTURED DATA — No JSON-LD, no schema of any kind. Zero.
  • ❌ TEXT CONTENT — Homepage has ~222 characters of visible text — essentially nothing.
  • ❌ JS RENDERING — All content depends on Duda's JavaScript loader.
  • ❌ NO BLOG / CONTENT — Zero blog posts, zero articles, zero content strategy.
  • ❌ REVIEWS NOT VISIBLE — Reviews are JS-rendered and not schema-marked up.
  • ❌ STALE CONTENT — Contact page untouched since August 2022 (4 years).
  • ❌ ONLY 6 PAGES — Tiny site footprint. Competitors with 20+ pages will dominate.

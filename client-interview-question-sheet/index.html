📋 Client Copy Interview — Question Sheet

90 minutes · Record the call · Work through topics, not pages

🔴 WHY They Started 20 min

🎯 Goal: Uncover the personal/emotional story that becomes the About page
What personal experiences or background influenced your decision to start this business?
Is there a particular story or moment that made you realize this was the right path?
How does your business make a difference in the lives of your clients or the community?
Did you see a problem in your industry that you could solve?
What three words would you like people to associate with your brand?
If someone left you a 5-star review, what would you want it to say?
How do you see things differently from the mainstream in your industry?
Where do you see your business in five years?

👥 PEOPLE 10 min

🎯 Goal: Listen for credentials, awards, certifications — About page trust signals
Can you list the people on your team with their roles? (Listen for: degrees, certs, awards, years of experience, personal interests)

🎯 WHO They Serve 15 min

🎯 Goal: Build the ideal client profile for targeting and messaging
★ MUST If you hosted a party for your ideal clients — who are they? What are they like?
Tell me about a recent client who perfectly embodied your target audience.
What common goals or problems do clients have when they come to you?
What is the most important thing your clients need from you?
★ MUST Can you share a specific success story or testimonial?
How do clients feel after experiencing your results?

🔧 WHAT They Do 25 min

🎯 Goal: Get every service name, description, and the "secret sauce" for each
★ MUST What's your overall "secret sauce"? What makes you different?
What's one unconventional method you've used to get great results?
★ MUST Let's go through each service — name + brief description for each.
Are there seasonal or special services you offer certain times of year?
How do you stay connected with clients throughout their journey?

📍 WHERE + Contact 5 min

🎯 Goal: Exact details — these go straight to the Contact page
★ MUST Where do you offer your services? (All services in all locations?)
★ MUST What is your phone number?
★ MUST What is your address?
★ MUST What is your public email address?
What are your business hours?

📋 HOW — Client Onboarding 10 min

🎯 Goal: Understand the customer journey — goes into Services page
How do clients typically reach out? (Phone, walk-in, email, website?)
Do you have an online booking system?
Do you have a client welcome pack or onboarding process?
How long do projects or repairs take on average?
What should clients know to set the right expectations?

📧 LEAD NURTURING 15 min

🎯 Goal: Harvest FAQs, myths, and objections for the email drip + lead magnet
Are there any prerequisites clients need to work with you?
Is there anything that would disqualify a client?
★ MUST What are the most frequently asked questions you get? (Try for 5)
What common problems do clients come to you with?
What are the biggest myths or misconceptions in your industry?
Do you find you need to educate leads before they become clients?
⏱️ Suggested Pacing: WHY+People (25 min) → WHO (15 min) → WHAT (25 min) → WHERE (5 min) → HOW (10 min) → Lead Nurturing (10 min)

📝 Interviewer Reminders: • Get the client talking about WHY they started — practical, personal, and emotional reasons • Listen for credentials (degrees, certs, awards, years in business) • Capture the client's OWN WORDS — they make the best copy • Flag anything that sounds like a contradiction • Record the call — the transcript is the source material for everything

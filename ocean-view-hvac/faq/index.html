Common questions

Straight answers about service, quotes, and what to expect.

Do you offer 24/7 service?

Phone support is open 24/7 all year. Call or text and we will get back to you.

Do you charge for repair quotes?

Repair quotes are free. We explain what we found and what we recommend, and nothing starts until you approve the quote.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. We are licensed and insured for heating, cooling, repairs, installation, and maintenance on all brands of HVAC equipment.

What brands do you work on?

All brands. We work closely with Amana, Goodman, Daikin, and Mitsubishi, and we are happy to quote any brand you prefer.

Do you work on commercial buildings?

Yes. We serve offices, schools, businesses, and real estate as well as homes.

What types of systems do you repair?

Split and ducted systems, mini splits, boilers, heat pumps, and gas and electric heating.

Why do you look for the cause instead of just replacing parts?

Replacing a part without finding out why it failed leads to repeat breakdowns. We investigate first so the problem does not come back.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Norfolk, Virginia Beach, Portsmouth, Chesapeake, Suffolk, and Hampton. See our service areas page for details.

Ready for a comfortable space?

Call or text any time, or send a request. Repair quotes are free, and nothing starts until you approve it.

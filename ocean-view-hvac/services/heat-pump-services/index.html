Heat pump services

Repair, installation, and maintenance for the system that heats and cools.

A heat pump moves heat instead of making it, which is why it is an efficient choice in Norfolk, where winters are mild. One system handles both seasons.

We quote any brand you prefer, and we work closely with Amana, Goodman, Daikin, and Mitsubishi.

Signs you need this

  • Ice on the outdoor unit that does not clear
  • Backup heat runs constantly
  • Heating bills higher than they should be
  • The system does not switch between heating and cooling

What is included

  • Defrost control and reversing valve repair
  • Refrigerant charge and leak repair
  • Backup heat strip testing
  • New heat pump installation

Frequently asked questions

Do heat pumps work in cold weather?

Modern heat pumps work well in our climate. On the coldest nights, backup heat can help, and it should only run when needed.

Is ice on my heat pump normal?

A thin frost that melts in a defrost cycle is normal. Thick ice that stays is not, and it deserves a service call.

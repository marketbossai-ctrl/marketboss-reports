What Needs Work — Refinements, Not Rebuilds

  • ⚠ HOMEPAGE H1 — "Marketing Agency in Virginia Beach, VA" is accurate but generic. It does not say what makes Market Boss AI different. Consider: "Local SEO & Google Business Profile Agency in Virginia Beach" or incorporate the map-pack angle that the calculator targets.
  • ⚠ PACKAGES PAGE — At 398 words with no schema markup and a thin meta description ("Market Boss AI packages and pricing"), this is the weakest page on the site. It is the page that closes the sale and it deserves more depth: why three tiers, who each is for, testimonials, FAQ schema.
  • ⚠ BLOG CADENCE — Last post was August 5. It is now August 24 (19 days without new content). Google rewards consistency. Aim for 1 post per week minimum, even if shorter.
  • ⚠ PILLAR CONTENT — 19 blog posts with no hub-and-spoke structure. There is no single definitive guide that all posts link back to. A "Complete Hampton Roads Local SEO Guide" pillar page (the one in the sitemap) should be expanded and every blog post should internally link to it.
  • ⚠ AREA+SERVICE COMBO PAGES — 77 pages is an impressive footprint, but volume creates risk. If any of these have thin or duplicative content (same paragraph with city name swapped), Google will ignore them. Audit a random sample of 5-10 to verify each is genuinely unique.
  • ⚠ NO VIDEO CONTENT — Video is the fastest-growing content format for local SEO. A 2-minute "What is a Google Business Profile?" video embedded on the GBP service page would differentiate and increase dwell time.
  • ⚠ EMAIL NURTURE — The calculator captures email, but what happens after that? An automated nurture sequence ("day 1: your revenue report," "day 3: case study," "day 7: free audit offer") would convert calculator signups into consultations.
  • ⚠ GBP LISTING — Could not verify live (Google Places API key pending). Confirm the GBP is claimed, category is "Marketing Agency" or "SEO Service," all services are listed, service areas match the website cities, reviews are being responded to, and Google Posts are going out weekly.

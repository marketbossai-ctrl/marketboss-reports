Balance Wash and Fold: The Mobile Laundry Service That Puts the Balance Back Between Life and Laundry

Virginia Beach Spotlight · August 26, 2026

Picture this: you leave a bag of dirty laundry on your front porch in the morning, and by the next afternoon, it comes back to you washed, dried, folded, and ready to put away. No laundromat. No quarters. No spending your Saturday staring at a dryer. That is the promise of Balance Wash and Fold, a mobile laundry service founded by Desiree Huff and serving families all across Hampton Roads since 2019.

Desiree describes her business simply: it is like Uber for your clothes. She and her team drive to your home, pick up your laundry, take it to a local laundromat, weigh it, send you a digital invoice, and return your clothes clean and folded. The entire transaction runs through your phone, and everything from detergent to fabric softener is handled on their end. You never touch a machine, a coin, or a dryer sheet.

A Service Born From Real Life Experience

Balance Wash and Fold did not start as a business plan in a boardroom. It started in a laundromat, where Desiree spent two to three hours every single week with her children, watching loads spin and wondering why something so time consuming did not have a better answer. She knew firsthand how much of a burden laundry is for a busy household, because she was living it.

That experience shaped everything about how the business operates. Desiree is a single mother of two, and she built this company with the understanding that families do not need one more chore. They need a partner. They need someone who will show up, communicate, and treat their clothes with the same care they would give their own. Customers like Amber, who wrote that the customer service is amazing and that communication is spot on, will tell you that is exactly what they get. Her laundry was picked up and delivered with quality and care, and that level of consistency is what keeps people coming back.

Tericka, a customer in Chesapeake, describes the experience the same way. She says it is a pleasure using Balance Wash and Fold, that the customer service and quality of work is top tier, and that the owner is so polite and easy to work with. She adds that her clothes are always returned looking and smelling great. When you hear the same words from customer after customer, you know it is not luck. It is a standard.

Certified, Trusted, and Growing Beyond the Basics

What sets Balance Wash and Fold apart from app based laundry services is the personal touch combined with real credentials. Desiree holds a SWAM certification as a small, women owned, and minority owned business, and she recently added a Certified Linen and Laundry Management certification to her resume. That certification opened up an entirely new side of the laundry world, one that goes beyond washing and folding and into professional protocol, policy, and training.

On top of residential clients, the business serves commercial customers including Airbnbs, gyms, and salons, and has even expanded into government contracting. Balance Wash and Fold currently holds two contracts with military bases in the area, work that requires consistency, documentation, and a level of professionalism most laundry services never have to think about. Ray, a customer in Virginia Beach, summed it up simply. He called the service highly professional, saying they did an outstanding job and that he would definitely use them again. Friendly service, delivered on time, every time. That is the reputation she has built.

Desiree is quick to credit where the growth comes from. She says her clients are literally sent by God, arriving exactly when she needs them, and she has learned to trust that flow. She can go a week without a single client and then the next week every single one shows up and makes up for the quiet stretch. It is a rhythm she has learned to rely on.

Flexible Packages That Fit Your Life

Balance Wash and Fold is not a one size fits all operation. For those who just want occasional help, there is a simple pay as you go option at $1.95 per pound with free next day pickup and delivery. Families who want regular support can choose from monthly packages with weekly or bi weekly pickups, each including sorting, premium detergent, and next day delivery. There is even a Baby Ready package that preps everything before the baby arrives, and a Fourth Trimester Support option for new moms, with gentle, fragrance free, baby safe washing handled separately from household laundry.

Add ons include hand steaming for dress shirts, silk, and gowns, plus professional ironing for uniforms, chef coats, and medical scrubs. Whether you need your chef whites pressed for service or a comforter washed the week before guests arrive, Balance Wash and Fold has a plan and a price for it.

A Business Built on Faith, Family, and Follow Through

Desiree is a serial entrepreneur with her hands in more than one pie. In addition to the laundry business and the government contracts, she runs a tax business with a partner, helping people make the leap from side gig to official LLC. But Balance Wash and Fold remains the heart of it all, the business she built from the ground up with her own two hands and her own lived experience.

When you schedule a pickup, you will notice the difference quickly. Someone answers your text. Your pickup happens when it was promised. Your clothes come back clean, folded, and delivered. It sounds simple, but as anyone who has ever waited on a missing load or a folded pile that looks like a wrinkled mess knows, simple is exactly what most laundry services fail to deliver. Balance Wash and Fold gets it right, week after week.

If you are tired of giving up your evenings and weekends to laundry, or you are a business owner who wants one less thing on your plate, Balance Wash and Fold is worth a call. Put the balance back between life and laundry, and let your clothes come to you.

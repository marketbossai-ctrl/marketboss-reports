Meet Richard Rasmus and MyAIPathways

When Richard Rasmus first experimented with ChatGPT 3.5 back in 2023, he knew he was looking at something that would change the way businesses operate. What he did not expect was how quickly that realization would turn into a company.

Richard is a leadership development executive coach and the executive director of a nonprofit in Loudoun County. He is also someone who recognizes a gap in the market when he sees one. Large consulting firms charge rates that put AI implementation out of reach for most small and medium businesses. Meanwhile, the smaller organizations that make up the backbone of Hampton Roads are left wondering if AI is something they can ever realistically use.

MyAIPathways exists to answer that question with a clear yes. Richard founded the company to help small and medium businesses identify where artificial intelligence can make an immediate impact, then guides them through the process of putting it to work. No six figure retainers. No one size fits all packages. Just a practical, people first approach to a technology that is evolving faster than most business owners can keep up with.

AI Built for People, Not Instead of Them

At MyAIPathways, the mission is straightforward: empower organizations and their employees to work smarter with AI, not replace them with it. The company believes that AI should be a tool in the hands of people, enhancing human creativity, productivity, and decision making. They do not advocate for eliminating jobs in favor of automation. Instead, they teach teams how to integrate AI thoughtfully and ethically into their existing workflows, keeping humans at the heart of the business.

Richard is quick to address the elephant in the room. When people hear artificial intelligence, they often worry about losing their jobs. MyAIPathways takes a different position. The goal is not to replace people, but to free them up to do the parts of their job that actually require human judgment.

Every organization is unique, and adopting AI is not a one size fits all process. That is why MyAIPathways starts with your needs, your team, and your goals. They build foundational knowledge and small wins first, before scaling up. From choosing the right tools to designing practical processes, the focus is on sustainable, approachable solutions that truly support and elevate people.

How MyAIPathways Works

The process starts with a conversation. Richard and his team sit down with a business owner or leader and ask a series of questions about what is actually happening inside the organization. What are the pain points? Where is the friction? What opportunities are sitting on the table that nobody has had time to pursue?

They take that conversation, anonymize it, run it through their AI tools, and come back within a week with a targeted set of recommendations for a pilot project. That report is free. There is no obligation. If the business likes the recommendations, they can move forward. If they find someone else who can do it better or more affordably, Richard is genuinely happy for them.

That kind of low pressure approach is rare in a field that is still figuring itself out. But it is central to how MyAIPathways operates. The team is not trying to force square pegs into round holes. They are listening first, then matching the right tools to the right problems.

Growing Together: A Social Impact Model

MyAIPathways believes that AI consulting should strengthen communities as well as organizations. That philosophy is built into the company's talent model. For entry level professionals, MyAIPathways provides real world project experience that helps them become stronger job candidates. For experienced subject matter experts, it offers flexible opportunities to mentor and guide projects outside the traditional full time workforce.

This blended approach benefits clients with unparalleled affordability and practical expertise, while expanding career pathways for people at every stage. The result is a team that brings a diverse range of perspectives and skills to every project, from recent graduates with strong AI fundamentals to seasoned executives who want to stay involved without the full time commitment.

What AI Actually Looks Like for a Small Business

Richard points to a common example. A home service company — HVAC, plumbing, electrical — generates field reports that pile up on someone's desk. An experienced employee has to go through every single one, looking for errors before invoices go out to customers. That process is tedious, repetitive, and takes time away from higher value work.

MyAIPathways trains their AI tools to learn what that experienced person looks for. What used to take a human reviewing 100 pieces of paper can be reduced to reviewing 10. The human still makes the final call. The errors that would have slipped through are caught. The employee spends their time on work that actually matters.

Another example is bid review. Many businesses have internal rules for how proposals should be structured. MyAIPathways can take those rules and check draft proposals against them, flagging inconsistencies before they go out to a potential client. The same principle applies: the AI handles the repetitive drudgery, and the human stays in control of the outcome.

Who MyAIPathways Serves

The company works with a wide range of clients, but Richard's background in operations gives him a natural affinity for home service industries, construction, and field service organizations. These are businesses where every hour of the day is billable, and any time saved on paperwork translates directly to more time spent serving customers.

MyAIPathways also serves nonprofits, professional services firms, and any organization that wants to explore what AI can do for them without making a major upfront investment. The free consultation and report means there is no risk in having the conversation.

What Makes MyAIPathways Different

There are plenty of AI consultants out there. What sets MyAIPathways apart is the combination of affordability, flexibility, and genuine curiosity about what each client actually needs. They are not pushing a product. They are not trying to upsell a subscription. They are having a conversation, building a report, and letting the client decide what happens next.

Richard describes it simply: "We are not desperate for work that we are going to get over our skis. If there is a project that requires a specialist, we will find someone to collaborate with. We are happy to share the burden."

That collaborative, low ego approach is exactly what small business owners in Hampton Roads need. AI is not going anywhere. The question is whether local businesses will have a trusted partner to help them navigate it. MyAIPathways is making a strong case that they should be that partner.

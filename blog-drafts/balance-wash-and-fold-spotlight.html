Balance Wash and Fold: Virginia Beach's Mobile Laundry Service That Picks Up More Than Just Clothes

Desiree Huff turned a laundromat frustration into a certified, faith-driven business serving families, military bases, and everyone in between.

📅 August 28, 2026 ⏱ 6 min read 🏷️ Mobile Laundry · Small Business · Virginia Beach

Desiree Huff did not start Balance Wash and Fold because she saw a gap in the market. She started it because she was living in the gap.

A single mom with two kids, she spent two to three hours every week sitting in a laundromat. Watching the machines spin. Entertaining a toddler. Wishing there was another way. And eventually, she asked the question that every great business is built on: "Why isn't there a service for this?"

In 2019, she answered her own question and launched Balance Wash and Fold — a mobile laundry service that operates like Uber for your clothes. You leave your laundry outside your door. They pick it up, wash it, dry it, fold it, and bring it back. You never set foot in a laundromat again.

Six years later, that simple idea has grown into something far bigger than Desiree originally imagined. Balance Wash and Fold now holds government contracts with two military bases, carries a SWAM certification (Small Women and Minority-Owned) and a Certified Linen and Laundry Management credential, and is building a reputation as one of the most personalized laundry services in Hampton Roads.

From Laundromat Frustration to a Full-Time Mission

Desiree's path to entrepreneurship was not a straight line. She was working full-time, raising two children, and spending her weekends in a laundromat — the same cycle millions of parents know by heart. The difference is that she decided to do something about it.

She did the research. She visited laundromats across Virginia Beach, checking prices at Holland Road versus Lynnhaven, calculating margins, and building a model that would work. She knew she did not want a storefront — no broken machines to fix, no lease to worry about. She wanted a mobile business that could go where the customers were.

When she launched, mobile laundry was almost unheard of in Hampton Roads. Poplin and Hampr did not exist yet. The market was wide open, and Desiree walked through it.

How Balance Wash and Fold Works

The process is simple. Clients leave their laundry outside their home — bagged and ready to go. Balance Wash and Fold picks it up, takes it to the nearest laundromat in the client's area, weighs the items, and sends an invoice. Once the invoice is paid, the laundry goes in the machine. Wash, dry, fold, deliver. The business provides the detergent.

What makes it different: "You know who you are getting," Desiree says. "When you book with our competition, they send random people. With me, you know me, you can text me anytime."

Desiree runs the business herself alongside two contractors who cover the Suffolk, Portsmouth, and Chesapeake areas. All bookings come through her — she controls the quality, the communication, and the experience. That personal touch is what keeps clients coming back week after week.

She offers monthly packages, bi-weekly service, weekly pickups, and specialized options like postpartum/newborn packages and military service member plans. For her consistent weekly clients, a single customer can generate roughly $1,000 a month in profit — a model that shows just how scalable a mobile laundry business can be with the right systems in place.

Government Contracts and Certification

What started as a residential service has expanded into something Desiree never expected. She now holds two government contracts with military bases in Hampton Roads — work that came through a partner who handles the bidding process while her business does the actual service.

To qualify for those contracts, she earned her Certified Linen and Laundry Management certification — a credential she did not even know existed until she needed it. That certification has opened doors she had not planned for. "If I just wanted to train people on making sure they have the right policies in place for their laundromat, I could stop washing and just do that," she says. "There are so many avenues here."

Her SWAM certification (Small Women and Minority-Owned) gives her an additional edge in securing contracts and partnerships that larger competitors cannot access.

A Serial Entrepreneur With a Servant's Heart

Balance Wash and Fold is not Desiree's only business. She also runs a tax preparation and bookkeeping service that operates year-round — not just during tax season — and specializes in helping people transition from side gigs to official LLCs. It is the same path she walked herself, and she wants to make it easier for others.

Her faith is woven into how she runs everything. She is a member of Rehoboth Baptist Church on Birdneck Road, and she says plainly that her clients are sent by God. "I can go a week without having any clients, and then the following week, all of those clients make up for what I did not have," she says. "It is a perfect flow. I never have too many where I am stressed out."

That trust in timing is paired with a practical drive. Her next goal is a dedicated work van — something she has already seen a preview of, when one client's laundry filled the front seat, backseat, and trunk of her personal car. "Okay, Lord, I see what you are showing me," she says. "That van is coming."

Why Balance Wash and Fold Is Different From the Apps

Mobile laundry apps have exploded in the last few years. Poplin, Hampr, and others have entered the Hampton Roads market, offering on-demand laundry services through gig workers. But Desiree is not worried.

Her business is built on a different foundation. She is certified. She is local. She is personally invested in every client relationship. Her contractors are part of a team, not strangers picked up from an app. When you book Balance Wash and Fold, you know who is coming to your home, and you can reach them by phone or text anytime.

That kind of trust is not something an app can replicate.

What's Next for Balance Wash and Fold

Desiree has been in business since 2019, and she has grown every year — not as fast as she would like, but steadily. She is actively looking for mentors and partners in the laundry industry, and she wants to keep expanding her government contracts while building her residential base.

The work van is the next milestone. Then more contractors. Then more bases. One client, one load, one relationship at a time.

If you are in Virginia Beach, Norfolk, Chesapeake, Portsmouth, or Suffolk and you are tired of spending your weekends in a laundromat, Balance Wash and Fold is worth a call. Desiree will answer — and she will probably ask how your kids are doing before she asks what you need washed.

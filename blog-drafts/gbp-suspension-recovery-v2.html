Your Google Business Profile Was Suspended — Here Is What Happened and How to Fix It

You open Google Maps to check your listing and it is gone. Not ranking lower. Gone. "Your Business Profile has been suspended." No warning. No explanation. Just a locked dashboard and a phone that stopped ringing.

If you are a plumber, HVAC contractor, electrician, or roofer in Hampton Roads, this is one of the most stressful things that can happen to your business. Your Google Business Profile is not just a listing — it is your digital storefront. When it disappears, so do your leads.

This guide walks you through the most common reasons for GBP suspensions, the step-by-step recovery process, and what Google's actual policies say. Every claim below is checked against Google's published Guidelines for representing your business on Google.

What Google's Policies Actually Say

Before we get into suspension recovery, it helps to understand exactly what Google requires. These are the rules from Google's own Business Profile guidelines:

  • One profile per business. Duplicate profiles cause problems with how your information displays on Google Maps and Search.
  • Your name must reflect your real-world business name — exactly as it appears on your storefront, website, stationery, and branding. No marketing taglines, store codes, or phone numbers in the name field.
  • Your address and service area must be accurate. Service-area businesses (contractors who travel to customers) must hide their address and use service areas instead.
  • Choose the fewest number of categories needed to describe your core business.
  • P.O. boxes and mailboxes at remote locations are not acceptable as business addresses.
  • Virtual offices and coworking spaces are not eligible for a Business Profile unless they have clear signage, receive customers during business hours, and are staffed during those hours.
  • If you run your business from a residential address, clear the address from your profile entirely.
  • Provide a phone number that connects to your individual business location.
✅ Verified from Google's official documentation. All of these rules are published in Google's Guidelines for representing your business on Google. Google updates these frequently, so checking the current version is always a good idea.

The Most Common Reasons Contractors in Hampton Roads Get Suspended

1. Address Violations

This is the most common issue for home service contractors. Google's policy is clear: service-area businesses — plumbers, electricians, HVAC companies, roofers — must hide their address in GBP settings and define their service area instead.

The mistake many Hampton Roads contractors make is displaying a physical address when they do not have a storefront customers can visit. This includes:

  • Using a residential home address
  • Using a P.O. box or UPS store mailbox
  • Listing a coworking space or virtual office as a storefront

Google's policy states: "If you run your business from your residential address, clear the address from your Business Profile." And: "P.O. boxes or mailboxes at remote locations aren't acceptable."

2. Category and Business Information Issues

Google says to "choose the fewest number of categories it takes to describe your overall core business." If a Norfolk electrician selects 6 categories including "Plumber," "Handyman," and "General Contractor," that signals to Google that the business information may not be accurate.

Your primary category should match your main service offering. Your business name, phone number, and website must be consistent across your GBP, your website, and all directory listings.

3. Prohibited Content or Behavior

Google requires that all profiles "avoid prohibited content and behavior." This is a broad category that covers review manipulation, misleading information, fake representations, and spam. While Google does not publish specific thresholds for review velocity or detection methods, the policy makes clear that any attempt to artificially inflate your reputation is grounds for suspension.

⚠️ What we don't know: Google does not publish specific review thresholds or suspension detection triggers. Claims from other websites about exact numbers (e.g., "3-5 reviews per month is the sweet spot") are not from Google. Take them with caution.

Step-by-Step GBP Recovery Process

Google's help center does not currently publish a dedicated "appeal a suspension" article with step-by-step instructions. The information below is based on Google's published policies, documented reinstatement processes from business owners, and our direct experience handling suspensions for Hampton Roads contractors.

1

Identify the Violation

Log into your GBP dashboard. The suspension notification will usually indicate the general area of the issue — address, business information, or policy violation. Compare your profile against the checklist in Section 1 above. The most common starting points: visible address on a service-area business, category mismatch, or business name that does not match your real-world branding.

2

Fix the Issue Before Appealing

Correction is required before Google will consider reinstatement. If you are a service-area business with your address visible, go to GBP settings → Info → hide your address → set your service area to the Hampton Roads cities you serve. If your categories are too broad, narrow them to the most specific fit. If your business name includes marketing slogans or location keywords, change it to match your legal business name.

3

Prepare Verification Documentation

Google will typically request documents to verify your business identity. Have these ready:

  • Virginia business license or professional license
  • Utility bill showing your business address (dated within 90 days)
  • Photos of your business location or service vehicles with visible branding
  • Photos of you or your team working at customer locations

These are standard verification documents Google uses to confirm that your business is a real, operating entity.

4

Submit the Appeal

In your GBP dashboard, locate the appeal option. Write a clear, professional explanation of what caused the violation and what you have done to fix it. Attach your supporting documentation. Google does not publish a guaranteed response time — reinstatement timelines vary significantly based on the complexity of the case.

5

Monitor and Follow Up

Check your GBP dashboard regularly. Do not submit multiple appeals — doing so can reset the review process. Some reinstatements resolve in a few days; others can take several weeks.

How to Prevent Suspensions Going Forward

Based on Google's actual policies, here is what you should and should not do to keep your profile active:

Do these every month:

  • Check that your address settings match Google's current guidelines — they update their policies frequently
  • Verify your business name, phone number, and website are identical on your GBP and your website
  • Keep your categories focused on your actual core services

Avoid these:

  • Do not use a virtual office, coworking space, P.O. box, or residential address as your storefront address unless you have a physical location where customers visit you during business hours
  • Do not create duplicate profiles for the same business
  • Do not include promotional content, phone numbers, or URLs in your business name field
  • Do not engage in review manipulation or incentivize reviews

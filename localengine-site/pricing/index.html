    FAQ

    Questions before you start?

    Is there a setup fee?

    No. Your first month reserves your spot in the build queue and covers strategy, design direction and technical setup.

    Who owns the domain?

    You do, always. You give us access and we handle DNS, SSL and the technical connections.

    Why is there a 6-month minimum?

    Good websites and local rankings improve through steady work over time. The 6-month minimum gives us room to build, refine and improve. After that your plan is month-to-month, and you can cancel with 30 days’ notice.

    How does the Local SEO add-on work?

    Local SEO is $499/mo on top of any website plan. Each month we work your Google Business Profile, build citations and local links, publish a new or improved page, and fix what Search Console flags. You get a Maps rank grid, weekly keyword tracking and a monthly report.

    Can I add or remove add-ons later?

    Yes. The website plan is the base. Add-ons can be added anytime and dropped after their minimum term without touching your website.

    What does support cover?

    Everyday updates like text changes, photo swaps and small content edits. Larger redesigns, new sections or advanced features are quoted before any work starts.

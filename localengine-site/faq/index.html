FAQ

Questions we hear a lot.

Do you guarantee results?

No, and be careful with anyone who does. We can’t control demand for your service or how a sale gets closed. We commit to the work: managing your website, Google presence and follow-up every month, and showing you exactly what we did.

How does the free preview work?

Tell us about your business and we build a real homepage around it. You review it before paying anything, then decide whether to have us build the rest.

Why do I need a website if Facebook is working?

Facebook keeps you in touch with people who already know you. A website is yours, and it’s what Google shows to people searching for your service right now.

What does the monthly price cover?

Design, hosting, SSL, security, backups, uptime monitoring, software updates and monthly edits. There’s no setup fee.

Do I own my domain?

Always. You give us access and we handle DNS, SSL and the technical connections.

Can I add or remove add-ons later?

Yes. The website plan is the base. Add-ons can be added anytime and dropped after their minimum term without touching your site.
